 # SQL Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data using SQL to extract meaningful business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, product categories, gender, and transaction patterns.

The analysis covers **10 key business questions**, using SQL techniques such as filtering, aggregation, grouping, date functions, window functions, and conditional logic.

## Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales on a Specific Date

Retrieved all sales transactions made on **November 5, 2022**.

### 2. Clothing Sales Analysis

Identified Clothing transactions where the **quantity sold was greater than 10** during **November 2022**.

### 3. Sales by Category

Calculated the **total sales for each product category** to compare category-level performance.

### 4. Customer Age Analysis

Calculated the **average age of customers** who purchased products from the **Beauty** category.

### 5. High-Value Transactions

Identified transactions where **total sales exceeded 1,000**.

### 6. Gender and Category Analysis

Calculated the **number of transactions by gender within each product category**.

### 7. Monthly Sales Analysis

Calculated the **average sales for each month** and identified the **best-selling month for each year**.

### 8. Top 5 Customers

Identified the **top five customers based on total sales generated**.

### 9. Unique Customers by Category

Calculated the **number of unique customers purchasing from each product category**.

### 10. Sales Shift Analysis

Classified transactions into different shifts based on the transaction time:

* **Morning:** ≤ 12
* **Afternoon:** > 12 and ≤ 17
* **Evening:** > 17

Calculated the **number of orders in each shift** to understand sales activity throughout the day.
